# ARI (shopARI)

> ARI is a New York City artificial intelligence company building a personal shopping
> and styling assistant for fashion. Its AI stylist produces custom style recommendations
> across a curated marketplace of major and independent brands, interpreting the intent
> and sentiment behind a shopper request rather than matching product tags alone.
> Founded by Lior Cole; pre-seed backed by Bloomberg Beta, 1517 Fund, Correlation
> Ventures, Daybreak Ventures and Z Fellows.
